Step 3: simple decoding (CPU, same notebook/run)
Positive versus negative, raw and cleaned, fixed class-weighted logistic model
(C=1), no hyperparameter search; dummy class-prior baseline.
Features: all 105 electrodes, 40-ms bins in 0–800 ms, or a single 224–304-ms
average. A separate -200–0-ms analysis is a contextual/timing control, not a clean
biological negative control: noncausal filtering and prior fixations can affect it.
Three deterministic outer grouped folds, separately by source sentence, normalized
word spelling, or reader. All occurrences of a held-out grouping identity remain
together, including across readers. Export overlap counts for the other grouping
identities so reader-held-out is not mistaken for unseen-word/sentence evaluation.
One word's variants are grouped conservatively by casefolded edge-punctuation-
stripped spelling, not lemma; morphological relatives may remain across folds.
Training-only median imputation and channel-feature scaling. Fit sees training
only. Fixed settings avoid selecting a best model from test scores.
Compare single fixations with disjoint within-reader/class averages of 20 and40
trials, formed AFTER the split. Remainders dropped and actual input/test counts
reported. This uses the known class to form test averages: not single-event
sentiment prediction. Missing-class/low-count pseudotrial folds explicitly marked
incomplete. No confidence/significance claim from treating folds/pseudotrials as
independent readers. Balanced accuracy chance50%; report macroF1, AUC, confusion
matrix, fold counts and reader/fold scores. Metadata-only logistic control uses
length, saved frequency and fixation timing with the same grouped folds.

Interpretation limits shared by all new classifiers
The saved cleaning/nuisance model and some matching decisions used each reader's
entire recording before these new splits. Thus cleaned held-out evaluations are
transductive and cannot establish a strictly isolated fit-on-training-only
preprocessing result. Raw signals also retain shared filtering/matching and can
contain ocular/contextual confounds. Preserve both results, flag pre-onset and
metadata separability, and keep unseen-word, unseen-sentence and unseen-reader
claims distinct. This new archive contains no neutral trials, so no three-class
or neutral/nonneutral experiment can be run from it without a separate archive.
Multiple fixed cases are exploratory; do not select the highest score as a
confirmed replication or adjust labels/cutoffs to obtain significance.
